1. 7-Day Replacement & Return Guarantee

At KAKA'S LIFE, customer satisfaction is paramount. You may request an exchange, replacement, or refund within 7 days of receiving your parcel under any of the following circumstances:

  • Damaged or Leaking Bottle: If the perfume bottle arrived cracked, broken, or leaking due to courier transit.
  • Defective Atomizer/Spray: If the mechanical spray nozzle fails to dispense properly upon first use.
  • Incorrect Fragrance Delivered: If the variant received differs from the fragrance you ordered.

2. Luxury Fragrance Seal & The Free Tester Benefit

Perfumes and eau de parfum are personal cosmetic items subject to strict health and hygiene regulations. Once a perfume bottle has been unsealed, sprayed, and handled, it cannot be safely re-bottled or re-sold.

Risk-Free Sampling via Complimentary Tester
To give you complete peace of mind, every full-size bottle of ELITE comes with a complimentary luxury sample tester. We encourage you to spray and experience the tester first! If the scent does not suit your preference, you can return the unopened, sealed 50ml bottle for a full exchange or refund without any penalty.

To be eligible for a change-of-mind exchange, the main product packaging must be intact, sealed in its original box, and free of physical scuffs.

3. How to Initiate a Return or Exchange (3 Easy Steps)

Our return process is designed to be hassle-free without complex paperwork:

  1. Take a Photo or Short Video: Capture clear pictures of the damaged bottle, leakage, or incorrect parcel alongside the courier airway bill label.
  2. Send to Our WhatsApp Support: Message our team at +92 328 7004634 with your Order ID and delivery city within 7 days of parcel arrival.
  3. Dispatch Replacement or Refund: Our representative will immediately book a free reverse courier pickup or provide instructions to return the item. Upon receipt, a fresh replacement or refund is processed immediately.

4. Refund Methods & Processing Timelines

Approved refunds are disbursed through your preferred Pakistani payment channel:

5. Order Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your order at zero cost if the parcel has not yet been collected by the courier:

  • Before Dispatch: Orders are generally packed within 6 to 12 hours. Notify our WhatsApp support at +92 328 7004634 immediately to cancel. Any prepaid amounts will be refunded 100%.
  • After Dispatch: Once handed over to courier riders with an active tracking code, orders cannot be cancelled mid-transit. The customer may initiate an exchange following delivery.

6. Non-Returnable Items & Exceptions

  • Bottles where more than 10% of the juice has been used.
  • Items returned past the 7-day reporting window.
  • Products purchased from unauthorized third-party resellers or imitation channels not affiliated with KAKA'S LIFE.
