1. Our Security Commitment

At KAKA'S LIFE — Maison De Parfum, founded by Hafiz Muhammad Mubeen in Lahore, Pakistan, we recognize that purchasing luxury fragrances online requires total confidence. We have engineered our web store, order checkout, and data processing workflows with bank-grade security principles.

Our security architecture is designed to prevent unauthorized access, ensure the privacy of customer contact and delivery details, guarantee transparent payment handling, and eliminate the risk of counterfeit perfume products.

Core Security Principle
KAKA'S LIFE adheres to the principle of minimal data collection. We only request information strictly necessary to process and safely deliver your fragrance order to your doorstep in Pakistan.

2. 256-Bit SSL/TLS Encryption & Transport Security

All communication between your device (browser or mobile phone) and our website servers is encrypted using high-grade Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / TLS 1.2) with modern 256-bit AES encryption.

  • HTTPS Everywhere: All requests are automatically upgraded to HTTPS, rendering data unreadable to third parties, ISPs, or malicious Wi-Fi snoops.
  • Encrypted Form Submissions: Customer delivery details, WhatsApp numbers, and payment verification receipts are transmitted over cryptographically signed channels.
  • Content Security & DDoS Mitigation: Our hosting infrastructure incorporates multi-layered web application firewall (WAF) protections to prevent distributed denial of service (DDoS) and injection attempts.

3. Safe Payment Protocols & Anti-Fraud Protection

We offer two trusted payment avenues designed to provide Pakistani shoppers with maximum safety and convenience:

A. Cash on Delivery (COD) Nationwide

With Cash on Delivery, you pay zero upfront money. You pay the courier rider in cash only when the parcel is physically handed over at your doorstep. To protect both our customers and our operations from bogus or prank bookings, our team conducts a quick verification via WhatsApp or telephone before dispatching any COD package.

B. Online Digital Payment (Sadapay, Meezan Bank)

For customers preferring direct digital payments, we utilize Pakistan's most trusted platforms:

  • Zero Financial Credential Storage: KAKA'S LIFE never asks for your bank password, debit/credit card CVV, OTP, or mobile wallet MPIN. Anyone asking for these credentials is an impersonator and not KAKA'S LIFE.
  • Account Verification: When transferring funds via Sadapay or Meezan Bank, you only send funds to our verified business account titled Muhammad Mubeen Mirza / KAKA'S LIFE.
  • Transaction Proof Verification: Uploading your transaction ID (TRX ID) and screenshot inside our checkout modal allows our verification team to match and authenticate the payment safely before shipping.
Payment Security Guarantee
If any digital transaction fails or double-charges due to bank network glitches, our team verifies bank reconciliation statements within 24 business hours and issues an immediate resolution or refund.

4. 100% Authentic Fragrance & Tamper-Evident Packaging

In the fragrance industry, counterfeit perfumes pose health hazards and cheat consumers. KAKA'S LIFE enforces rigorous authenticity controls:

  • Direct Sourcing: All raw oils, fragrance compounds, and formulations are sourced directly from authorized fragrance houses and certified suppliers.
  • Batch Production Control: Every production batch of ELITE (Pour Homme) and upcoming collections undergoes strict maturation and olfactory quality inspection in Lahore.
  • Tamper-Evident Luxury Seal: Our bottles are presented with tamper-evident sealing. If a customer receives a package with a broken outer safety seal, they are entitled to an immediate replacement.
  • Complimentary Tester Inclusion: We include a complimentary 5ml luxury tester sample with every bottle order so you can test and fall in love with the scent before unsealing the main bottle!

5. Customer Data Privacy & Confidentiality

Your personal information is strictly protected:

  • No Selling of Data: We will never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal names, phone numbers, or delivery addresses to third-party telemarketers or ad brokers.
  • Logistics Sharing Only: Your address and contact number are shared strictly with our contracted courier partners (such as Leopards Courier, TCS, Trax, or PostEx) for the single purpose of dispatching and delivering your package.
  • Secure Media Storage: Payment confirmation screenshots submitted during online checkout are stored in access-controlled environments and securely archived after payment reconciliation.

6. Responsible Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

KAKA'S LIFE welcomes reports from security researchers, ethical white-hat hackers, and developers who help maintain the security and safety of our online platform. If you discover a security vulnerability, we invite you to report it responsibly.

Responsible Reporting Guidelines:

  • Email full technical details, proof-of-concept steps, and screenshots directly to softwareengineeringdesigner@gmail.com with the subject line [Security Vulnerability Report].
  • Allow us reasonable time (up to 7 business days) to investigate and patch the vulnerability before making any public disclosure.
  • Do not access, modify, or delete customer orders or sensitive brand data.
  • Do not perform automated spam, denial of service (DoS/DDoS), or social engineering attacks against our staff or systems.
Safe Harbor & Hall of Appreciation
We will not pursue legal action against researchers who discover and report vulnerabilities in good faith following these guidelines. Confirmed valid security reports will receive formal recognition in our Security Hall of Thanks.
