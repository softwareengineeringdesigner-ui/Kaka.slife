1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies and HTML5 Local Storage are small, harmless data files saved on your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you visit web pages. They enable the website to remember your selections (such as keeping ELITE or Mist in your shopping cart) so you don't lose your chosen fragrances when switching pages or refreshing your browser.

2. Storage Technologies We Use

We maintain an ultra-lean, privacy-first web architecture:

  • Strictly Necessary Storage: Used to maintain shopping cart quantities, keep track of discount promotions, and coordinate the multi-step checkout modal. Without these, you could not complete an order online.
  • Functional Session Memory: Stores whether you have interacted with our WhatsApp chatbot greeting or closed a notification banner so you are not interrupted repeatedly.
  • No Cross-Site Ad Tracking: We do NOT deploy invasive third-party cross-site advertising trackers that monitor your activity on other websites.

4. Third-Party CDNs & Infrastructure

To deliver rapid page load speeds across Pakistan, we utilize trusted public Content Delivery Networks (CDNs):

  • Google Fonts: Loads typography (Cormorant Garamond and Montserrat) with high visual elegance.
  • Bootstrap CDN (jsDelivr): Loads standard styling frameworks and responsive grid libraries securely.
  • Vercel Edge Cloud: Provides global high-availability hosting and SSL certificate management.

5. How to Control or Clear Cookies in Your Browser

You have complete control over whether cookies and storage are saved on your device. You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-party cookies > Clear browsing data.
  • Apple Safari (iOS / Mac): Settings > Safari > Advanced > Website Data > Remove All.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data > Clear Data.
Impact of Disabling Local Storage
If you choose to disable local storage entirely, items added to your cart may reset upon page refresh. You can still order by messaging our WhatsApp hotline (+92 328 7004634) directly.
