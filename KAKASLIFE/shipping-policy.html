1. Nationwide Delivery Coverage

KAKA'S LIFE ships to every city, town, and district across all provinces of Pakistan, including:

  • Punjab: Lahore, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Bahawalpur, Sargodha, Sheikhupura, and surrounding districts.
  • Sindh: Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana, Mirpurkhas, Nawabshah, and more.
  • Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Peshawar, Abbottabad, Mardan, Swat, Kohat, Dera Ismail Khan, and beyond.
  • Balochistan: Quetta, Gwadar, Turbat, Khuzdar, and major commercial hubs.
  • Islamabad Capital Territory & AJK / Gilgit-Baltistan: Islamabad, Mirpur, Muzaffarabad, Gilgit, Skardu.

3. Shipping Rates & Free Delivery Conditions

Free Nationwide Delivery on ELITE & Orders Over Rs. 3,000
All orders of our signature masterpiece ELITE (Pour Homme 50ml) qualify for 100% Free Nationwide Delivery. Additionally, any shopping cart totaling Rs. 3,000 or more receives automatic free delivery anywhere in Pakistan.

For standalone items or individual orders totaling under Rs. 3,000, a subsidized flat shipping rate of Rs. 200 is applied to cover secure, insured courier logistics.

4. Authorized Courier Logistics Partners

We exclusively partner with licensed, reputable Pakistani courier networks:

  • Leopards Courier Service (Extensive nationwide network and fast COD remittance)
  • TCS Express (Priority commercial delivery)
  • Trax Logistics & PostEx (Real-time tracking and customer SMS delivery alerts)

5. Cash on Delivery (COD) Handover Protocol

For customers choosing Cash on Delivery:

  • The courier rider will collect the exact invoice amount in Pakistani currency before handing over the parcel.
  • Please keep the exact change ready upon receiving the delivery SMS notification to avoid rider delays.
  • Courier protocol in Pakistan requires payment prior to breaking the outer shipping carton seal. If upon opening the parcel the item is damaged or incorrect, you are fully protected by our 7-Day Return Policy.

6. Order Tracking & WhatsApp Updates

Once your order is picked up from our Lahore facility, a unique tracking number (CN / AWB number) is assigned. You will receive tracking details directly via SMS or our WhatsApp automated notification service.

You can also track your shipment at any time by messaging our WhatsApp support with your Order ID.

7. Damaged in Transit Guarantee

All KAKA'S LIFE shipments are packed in high-density bubble cushioning and bespoke rigid boxes to withstand transit. In the rare event that a bottle is cracked or broken in transit, contact us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery with photos, and we will dispatch an immediate replacement at zero cost.
