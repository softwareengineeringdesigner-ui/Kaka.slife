1. Introduction

Welcome to KAKA'S LIFE — Maison De Parfum ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, processing, and protection of personal data from visitors and customers accessing our official website at https://kakaslife.vercel.app.

By accessing our website, browsing our collections, or placing orders for our luxury fragrances, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To fulfill perfume orders and provide luxury customer care, we collect the following types of information:

  • Contact & Delivery Information: Full Name, Primary Mobile Number, WhatsApp Number, Shipping Address, City, Postal Code, and Province across Pakistan.
  • Order Details: Selected perfume products, bottle quantities, customized gift options, special delivery instructions, and timestamps.
  • Payment Information: For Cash on Delivery (COD), order totals due at delivery. For Online Payments, Transaction Reference ID (TRX ID), payment method (JazzCash, Easypaisa, or Bank Transfer), and customer-uploaded payment verification screenshot. We never collect or store banking passwords, OTPs, or credit card CVV codes.
  • Communications Data: Messages, feedback, or inquiries sent via our WhatsApp widget (+92 328 7004634) or official email.
  • Technical Telemetry: General anonymized device metrics, browser type, and interaction timestamps to optimize website responsiveness.
Zero Telemarketing Guarantee
KAKA'S LIFE has a strict zero-spam policy. Your mobile number will never be sold, rented, or distributed to automated marketing lists or third-party call centers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate commercial and customer-fulfillment purposes:

  • Order Processing & Packing: Preparing your bespoke fragrance package and tester sample with proper labeling.
  • Doorstep Delivery: Handing over accurate address details to verified Pakistani courier networks for nationwide transit.
  • Order Verification & Anti-Fraud: Confirming genuine COD orders via a quick verification message or call to prevent bogus bookings.
  • Customer Support: Resolving tracking inquiries, damaged-in-transit claims, or scent inquiries via WhatsApp.
  • Platform Enhancement: Monitoring website speed, navigation usability, and mobile checkout convenience.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We hold your trust with the highest regard. We only share customer information with trusted third parties under strict confidentiality:

  • Authorized Logistics Couriers: Delivery address and recipient contact details are shared exclusively with reputable Pakistani delivery services (such as Leopards Courier, TCS, Trax, or PostEx) for parcel transit.
  • Web Hosting & Security Infrastructure: Our web store is hosted on enterprise cloud infrastructure (Vercel) utilizing SSL encryption protocols.
  • Legal Compliance: We will only disclose personal records if formally compelled by lawful court order or statutory requirements under the laws of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website utilizes browser localStorage and minimal session cookies to remember items placed in your shopping cart and retain your viewing preferences as you navigate between pages.

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings at any time without restricting your ability to browse our fragrance catalog. For comprehensive details, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

6. Data Retention & Storage Security

Customer order records are retained only as long as necessary to complete shipment delivery, comply with taxation and accounting regulations, and support return/warranty claims (typically up to 12 months). Payment verification screenshots are permanently archived or deleted once bank reconciliation is finalized.

7. Your Privacy Rights

As our valued customer, you have full authority over your personal information:

  • Access & Review: You may request a summary of the personal details and orders on file under your contact number.
  • Correction: If your shipping address or phone number changes prior to parcel dispatch, you can request an instant update via WhatsApp.
  • Data Erasure: You have the right to request the deletion of your customer profile and historical contact details from our records once open orders are fulfilled.
